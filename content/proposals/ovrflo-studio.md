# Overview

OVRFLO STUDIO has an opportunity to create a website that reflects the quality and ambition of its creative work.

The new site will clarify the studio's offer, present its projects with greater impact and create a more confident path to project enquiries. It should feel distinctive and considered, while remaining clear and easy to navigate.

# Goals

- Clarify the studio's positioning, services and value.
- Present projects through compelling case studies.
- Improve the path from first impression to project enquiry.
- Build a flexible foundation for future growth.

# Optional Add-ons

- Additional custom page or case study: from AUD $1,500.
- Additional campaign landing page: from AUD $2,500.
- Photography, film, illustration or 3D: quote on request.
- Ongoing website optimisation: quote on request.

# Exclusions

- Full brand identity redesign and content production, unless specifically included.
- Custom business software, portals or advanced back-end systems.
- Ongoing content entry, maintenance or campaign management after the support period.
- Third-party subscriptions, hosting, fonts, plugins and legal advice.

# Payment & Terms

- All prices are in Australian dollars and exclude GST. Invoices are payable within 14 days.
- Payment milestones: Core 50% to commence / 50% before launch; Complete 40% / 30% / 30%; Flagship 30% / 30% / 25% / 15%.
- Two structured refinement rounds are included at each major design stage. Consolidated feedback and timely approvals keep the project on schedule.
- Third-party costs are paid directly by OVRFLO STUDIO. Work outside the agreed scope will be quoted separately or billed at AUD $150 per hour.
