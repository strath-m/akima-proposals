# Overview

Lambruk Pantry is entering a new stage with the opening of a new cafe in Ballina, renewed product production, growing wholesale interest and a stronger opportunity to build online sales through Shopify.

This project will redesign and refresh the Lambruk Pantry website into a clearer, warmer and more functional ecommerce experience that better reflects the quality of the brand while supporting the new cafe.

The goal is to create a website that feels like Lambruk Pantry: country luxury, homely, premium, practical and grounded in a strong story of family, farmers, regional produce and carefully made pantry products.

# Goals

- Refresh the Shopify website to better reflect the quality and story of Lambruk Pantry.
- Improve the ecommerce experience so customers can easily browse and purchase products.
- Create a dedicated cafe page and experience for Lambruk Pantry Ballina with clear booking functionality.
- Better communicate key product benefits including farm-direct sourcing, low sugar, preservative-free recipes and careful product testing.
- Improve the website structure, mobile experience and basic SEO foundation.
