# Overview

Yapper now serves studios, agencies and enterprise teams, but the brand and site grew organically, feature by feature. They still read as consumer-first, and that's slowing down enterprise sales.

The goal is an intentional brand that earns enterprise trust without losing Yapper's warmth. It should be the simple, transparent alternative to Higgsfield, carried through the main site and a dedicated /enterprise page.

# Goals

- Be the anti-Higgsfield, simple and clear where they're overwhelming, and transparent where they're opaque
- Earn enterprise trust without becoming sterile, corporate or developer-first
- Stay friendly for prosumers, like Canva, while making professional use obvious
- Give the sales team a dedicated /enterprise page to send after a first call
- Design that's effective, not just impressive, and that gets out of its own way

# FAQs

## Can we start with Option 01 and add the website later?

Yes. If you start with the identity, we'll hold the Option 02 price difference (USD $6,000) for 60 days after the identity is delivered, so moving on to the pages costs the same as booking them now.

## How involved can Sean be?

As involved as he likes. We'd love Sean in the strategy workshop and at every checkpoint, and work in progress lives in Figma so feedback happens in context, not in one big reveal.

## Will you redesign the wordmark or refine it?

We'll decide together in the strategy phase. If the current wordmark's character is worth keeping, we'll refine it. If it's holding the brand back, we'll redesign it. Either way, it's included.

## Do you build the pages?

We deliver 1:1 interactive prototypes and a component library ready for your team to build. If you'd rather we build them in Framer or Webflow, we'll quote that separately.

## What if you hire a founding designer during the project?

Everything is documented in the guidelines and organised Figma files, and we'll run a handover session so your new designer can pick it up straight away. Option 03 also includes help defining the role and reviewing candidates.
