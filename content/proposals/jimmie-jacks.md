# FAQs

## Will we lose our Google rankings?
No. Google ranks your web addresses and the content on them, not the platform. Every address stays the same or redirects to its new home, all content moves across, and we watch your rankings closely for 30 days after launch.

## Will our leads keep coming in?
Yes. The new enquiry form sends every lead into Keap, with the same tags, SMS consent wording and follow-up as today. Before we build it, we confirm the connection works with your Keap account. If it doesn't, we keep your current Keap form as it is. Either way, we test it end to end before launch and monitor it after, and the current site stays live until the switch.

## How hard is the admin area to use?
If you can write an email, you can use it. Log in, change a price or write a post, hit save, and it's live. The design is locked, so nothing can break, and every change can be rolled back.

## What will you need from us?
Access to your website and shop (WordPress admin and hosting), domain, Cloudflare, Google and Keap accounts, your logo files, a shared link to your photos and videos (Google Drive or iCloud), and feedback on each design round within 5 working days.

## What happens to the shop?
Nothing changes unless you want it to. It keeps running at jimmiejackgear.com and is linked from the new site's menu. Before launch, we'll check how your WordPress and hosting are set up to confirm the shop is fully separate from the main website, so retiring the old site has no effect on it.

## What are the two shop paths, and how do they compare?
The shop isn't part of the website rebuild. If you'd like to improve it, there are two ways to go, at an extra fee, now or later.

**Path A · Refresh the current shop on WordPress (our recommendation) · USD $1,200.** The Jimmie Jack story rewritten in your voice, colours and logo matched to the new website, a link back to the main site, and updates, backups and checkout checked. Your team keeps adding and editing products exactly as they do now.

**Path B · Move the shop to Shopify · USD $6,000, plus Shopify's monthly fee.** A new Shopify store designed to match the new brand, with your products, sizes and photos moved across, payments, shipping and pickup at the lodge set up, jimmiejackgear.com kept so the QR codes still work, and a handover session for your team.

| | Path A · WordPress | Path B · Shopify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Upfront cost | USD $1,200 | USD $6,000 |
| Monthly cost | Your current hosting | Shopify Basic, USD $29–39/month, plus card fees |
| Looks like the new website | Colours, logo and copy updated | Fully redesigned to match |
| Adding and editing products | As today in WordPress, which your team already knows | Shopify's admin, simpler and easier on a phone |
| Updates and security | WordPress and plugin updates needed regularly | Handled by Shopify |
| Checkout and payments | Your current card checkout, unchanged | Shopify Checkout, with Apple Pay and Google Pay |

With around 10 products sold mostly to lodge guests, Path A gives the best return today. Path B is worth revisiting if the gear range or online sales grow.

## Do we still need our current web team?
No. Once the new site is live, your team can make everyday updates themselves, so the monthly fee for small edits and your WordPress hosting can stop.

## What do you recommend?
The website rebuild, with the shop left as it is for now. It puts your budget where your bookings come from. If you want the shop to look like the new site, the WordPress refresh (Path A) is the best value for a range this size. Shopify is worth it later if gear sales grow.
