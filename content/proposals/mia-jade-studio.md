# FAQs

## What will you need from us?
- **Brand assets:** logo files, colour palette and fonts
- **Inspiration list:** the reference sites Mia has put together, with notes on what she likes about each
- **Shopify store set-up:** your Shopify account and plan, with the launch range added (products, variants, prices, collections, images and stock) before the build starts on 9 November, plus payments, shipping and tax settings. We build the theme and website on top of this
- **Copy:** product descriptions and key page copy (Mardi), with our page structure and word guides to write to. We'll do a light edit for flow and fit
- **Photography and video:** a shot list early on so we can design around it, then final images and video by 13 November
- **Size guide:** measurements for each style
- **Access:** collaborator access to your Shopify store and access to your domain
- **Policies:** shipping, returns, privacy and terms (we'll set up the pages; the wording is yours)
- **Point of sale:** setting up Shopify POS and any in-store hardware for the Byron Bay store
- **One point of contact:** Mardi for day-to-day decisions, with Mia and Andy joining the workshop and design sign-off

## Can we have something live before the store is ready?
Yes. A coming-soon page with email sign-up goes live in week 2, so you can start building your launch list and pointing people somewhere while the full store is built.

## What if our photos and video aren't ready in time?
We design around your shot list using placeholders, so design and build don't wait on the shoot. Final images drop in during the last build week. If anything runs late, we launch with the best available images and swap them in afterwards.

## Do we need a more expensive Shopify plan for colour swatches?
No. We use a workaround that links each colour swatch to the right product images, so you get the visual swatch experience without upgrading. It needs images tagged in a particular way when new products are added; we'll show you how, and it's something we can manage for you on Option 02.

## Why put everything on Shopify, including the shop?
At your stage, keeping the online store, point of sale and inventory in one place means stock stays accurate across both channels, with no systems that don't talk to each other and no double handling. Your team sets up the products and inventory in Shopify; we build the theme and website that display them.

## Can we sell overseas?
Yes, Shopify supports international currencies, shipping and duties. We'll set Phase 1 up so it's ready to extend, and build international selling in Phase 2 when you're ready.

## Which option do you recommend?
Option 02. It gets you launched for less upfront, and as a startup with big plans, having us track how customers use the site and keep improving it is where the long-term value is.
